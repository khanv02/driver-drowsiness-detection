# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of experience,
identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, dataset licenses, and research-participant boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help others learn.
- Keep project discussions relevant and avoid publishing sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or sexualized attention.
- Deliberate intimidation, disruption, impersonation, or sustained bad-faith behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Uploading private recordings, credentials, restricted datasets, or other material that
  violates consent, license, or repository policy.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project
meetings, and other spaces where someone represents the DrowsyAlert project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner, [@khanv02](https://github.com/khanv02).
If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact and
do not include personal or sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly
as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
